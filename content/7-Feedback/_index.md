### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always willing to help whenever I encounter difficulties, even outside working hours. Everyone is supportive, and I always feel comfortable asking questions or sharing ideas. However, I think it would be nice to have more social gatherings activities to strengthen relationships.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor provides very detailed guidance, explains clearly when I don't understand, and always encourages me to ask questions. The admin team supports administrative tasks, provides necessary documents, and creates favorable conditions for me to work effectively. I especially appreciate that the mentor allows me to try and solve problems myself instead of just giving the answer.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The tasks I was assigned align well with the knowledge I learned at university, while also introducing me to new areas I had never encountered before. This allowed me to both strengthen my foundational knowledge and gain practical cloud and development skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During the internship, I learned many new skills such as AWS cloud services, containerization and deployment workflows, project management tools, teamwork skills, and professional communication in a corporate environment. The mentor also shared valuable real-world experiences that helped me better plan my career path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects each other, works seriously but still keeps things enjoyable. When there are urgent tasks, everyone works together and supports one another regardless of their position. This made me feel like a real part of the team, even as an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
Overall, I am happy with the support and benefits provided by the program. However, I would like to give some feedback on the approval of company-visit sessions. According to the program rules, interns need at least 10 approved sessions to come to the company, and I registered for more than 30 sessions but only 9 of them were approved. I feel that the approval process was not entirely fair, and I hope that in the future the team can review the approval criteria and process so that all interns have fair and equal opportunities.

### Additional Questions
- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?  
Meeting and connecting with many new people, as well as joining the vibrant and energetic events organized during the internship. Those moments made my time here memorable and enjoyable.
- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?  
The approval process for company-visit sessions. As I shared above, I registered for more than 30 sessions but only 9 were approved, which was below the minimum of 10 sessions required. I hope the criteria and process can be reviewed to be fairer and more transparent for all interns.
- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?  
Yes, definitely. The program provides great learning opportunities in cloud computing and AI, strong support from mentors and the admin team, and a friendly, open working environment where everyone is willing to help each other.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations
- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?  
I would suggest reviewing and improving the session approval criteria to ensure fairness for everyone, and organizing more team bonding activities so that interns can connect with each other and with the team more often.
- Would you like to continue this program in the future?  
Yes, I would love to. The experience has been very valuable, and I hope to keep learning and growing together with the FCAJ team.
- Any other comments (free sharing):  
I am truly happy and grateful to be a part of FCAJ. I hope the program will continue to grow and bring great opportunities to many future interns.
